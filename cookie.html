1. Introduction

This Cookie Policy explains how ("we," "us," or "our") uses cookies and similar tracking technologies on our website. This policy should be read together with our Privacy Policy and Terms of Service.

By using our website, you consent to the use of cookies in accordance with this policy. If you do not agree to our use of cookies, you should adjust your browser settings accordingly or not use our website.

2. What Are Cookies?

Cookies are small text files that are placed on your computer, phone, tablet, or other device when you visit a website. Cookies are widely used to make websites work more efficiently, remember preferences, improve security, measure performance, and provide information to website owners.

Cookies can include session cookies, which expire when you close your browser; persistent cookies, which remain on your device for a set period or until deleted; first-party cookies, which are set by the website you are visiting; and third-party cookies, which are set by another domain.

3. Types of Cookies We Use

3.1 Essential Cookies

These cookies are necessary for the website to function properly. They may support security, network management, accessibility, form handling, cookie preference storage, and basic website operation. You may not be able to opt out of essential cookies through our website.

Examples may include cookie consent preferences and session management while browsing.

3.2 Analytics Cookies

These cookies help us understand how visitors interact with our website by collecting and reporting information about traffic, page views, user behavior, technical performance, and optimization opportunities. Analytics information helps us improve the site experience and provider connection flow.

Examples may include Google Analytics or performance monitoring tools.

3.3 Functional Cookies

These cookies enable enhanced functionality and personalization. They may remember preferences, temporarily store form information, support phone or contact interactions, or help maintain a smoother request experience.

Examples may include language preferences, form data, and interaction preferences.

3.4 Marketing Cookies

These cookies may be used to understand campaign performance, measure referral sources, track advertisement engagement, and help display or evaluate relevant marketing. Marketing cookies may be set by us or by third-party advertising or analytics partners.

Examples may include advertising network cookies, call tracking tools, and social media or campaign measurement technologies.

4. Third-Party Cookies

We may use third-party services that set cookies or similar technologies on your device. These may include analytics providers, advertising platforms, call tracking services, form tools, security tools, and social media platforms. These third parties may collect information about your online activities across different websites according to their own policies.

We do not control third-party cookies. You should review the privacy and cookie policies of the relevant third-party services for more information.

5. How to Manage Cookies

You have the right to accept or reject non-essential cookies where required by law. Most web browsers automatically accept cookies, but you can usually modify your browser settings to decline, block, or delete cookies.

5.1 Browser Settings

You can control cookies through browser settings in common browsers such as Google Chrome, Mozilla Firefox, Safari, and Microsoft Edge. Browser settings may allow you to delete existing cookies, block future cookies, or receive alerts before cookies are stored.

5.2 Opt-Out Tools

You may also be able to opt out of certain third-party analytics or advertising cookies through tools such as Google Analytics Opt-Out, the Digital Advertising Alliance, or the Network Advertising Initiative.

Note: Blocking or deleting cookies may affect your experience on our website. Some features, forms, analytics, security functions, or request flow features may not work properly if cookies are disabled.

6. Do Not Track Signals

Some browsers include a "Do Not Track" (DNT) feature. Because there is currently no consistent industry standard for how DNT signals should be interpreted, our website does not currently respond to DNT browser signals or mechanisms.

We may update this Cookie Policy from time to time. When we make changes, we will post the updated Cookie Policy on this page and revise the "Last updated" date above.
