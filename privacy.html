Privacy Policy

This policy explains how may collect, use, disclose, and safeguard information submitted through this water damage provider connection platform.

Last updated: January 2025

1. Introduction

("we," "us," or "our") is committed to protecting your privacy. This Privacy Policy explains how we collect, use, disclose, and safeguard information when you visit our website, contact us, or request connection with independent local water damage restoration providers.

Please read this Privacy Policy carefully. By accessing or using our website, you agree to the practices described in this policy. If you do not agree with this policy, please do not use our website.

2. Information We Collect

2.1 Information You Provide to Us

We collect information that you voluntarily provide when you fill out contact forms, call us, communicate with us, request provider options, request estimates, or submit details about a water damage situation. This may include your name, email address, phone number, property location, service needs, project or damage details, messages, and any other information you choose to provide.

2.2 Automatically Collected Information

When you visit our website, we may automatically collect certain information about your device and browsing behavior, including IP address, general location data, browser type and version, operating system, pages visited, time spent on pages, referring website addresses, and device information.

2.3 Cookies and Tracking Technologies

We may use cookies, web beacons, analytics tools, call tracking, and similar technologies to collect information about browsing activities, site performance, referral requests, and user interactions. For more details, please see our Cookie Policy.

3. How We Use Your Information

We use the information we collect to respond to inquiries, process provider connection requests, communicate with you, share submitted request details with independent local providers or service partners, improve website performance, measure analytics, maintain records, support legal compliance, prevent fraud or misuse, and operate our referral platform.

Where required by law, we will seek consent before using information for marketing communications. You may opt out of marketing communications as described in this policy or in the message you receive.

4. Information Sharing and Disclosure

We do not sell, trade, or rent personal information to third parties. We may share information with independent service providers, service partners, vendors, analytics or technology providers, legal or regulatory authorities when required, parties involved in a business transfer, or other parties with your consent.

When you request provider options, submitted information may be shared with independent providers or service partners so they can contact you about your request. These providers are independent businesses and maintain their own privacy practices, pricing, licensing, insurance, availability, and work terms.

5. Data Security

We implement reasonable technical and organizational measures intended to protect personal information, which may include SSL encryption, secure servers, access controls, and periodic security reviews. However, no method of transmission or storage is 100% secure, and we cannot guarantee absolute security.

6. Your Privacy Rights

Depending on your location, you may have rights to access, correct, delete, or receive a copy of your personal information, opt out of marketing, object to certain processing, or request that we limit certain uses of your information. To exercise these rights, contact us using the details below.

Our website is not intended for children under 13. We do not knowingly collect personal information from children under 13. If you believe we have collected information from a child under 13, please contact us and we will take appropriate steps to delete such information.

8. Third-Party Links

Our website may contain links to third-party websites or resources. We are not responsible for the privacy practices, security, or content of external sites. Review third-party privacy policies when you visit their websites or communicate with independent providers.

To exercise these rights, contact us using the details below.

Your information may be transferred to and processed in countries other than your country of residence. By using our website, you understand that information may be processed in the United States or other locations where we or our service providers operate.

11. Data Retention

We retain personal information for as long as reasonably necessary to fulfill the purposes described in this policy, respond to requests, maintain business records, comply with legal obligations, resolve disputes, and enforce agreements.
